Our Story

25 Years of Cooking with Heart

Founded in 1998 by Chef Rajan, Spice Garden was born from a belief that authentic Tamil Nadu flavours deserve a real home. Every masala is ground fresh, every batter fermented overnight, every recipe unchanged for 25 years.

Today we serve over 500 families daily — from early morning tiffin to late-night dinner — with the same warmth and love as day one.
